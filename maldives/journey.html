🗺️ Travel tips

💵Carry US dollars — widely accepted. Local currency (Rufiyaa) used on local islands.
📱Get a Dhiraagu or Ooredoo SIM at Velana airport — ~$5 for 3 GB data.
🕌Dress modestly on local islands — cover shoulders and knees when walking through the village.
⏰Speedboats run on Maldivian time — confirm departure times with your guesthouse the night before.
🎒Pack light — domestic flights allow 20 kg checked, 5 kg carry-on.
☀️Best time to visit: November–April (dry season). May–October is rainier but cheaper.
