🗺️ Travel tips

💵 Carry US dollars — widely accepted throughout the Maldives. Local currency (Rufiyaa) used on local islands.
📱 Get a Dhiraagu or Ooredoo SIM at Velana airport — local data is cheap (~$5 for 3 GB).
🕌 Dress modestly when arriving at local islands — cover shoulders and knees when walking through the village.
⏰ Speedboats on local islands often run on Maldivian time — confirm departure times with your guesthouse the night before.
🎒 Pack light — luggage is limited on domestic flights (20 kg checked, 5 kg carry-on).
☀️ Best time to visit most atolls: November–April (dry season). May–October can bring rain but fewer tourists.
